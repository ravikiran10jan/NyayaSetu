NyayaSetu connects you with verified advocates across India for trusted legal consultation — by chat or call, in your language.

Find advocates by practice area (Family Law, Property Law, Criminal Law, Corporate Law, Employment Law, Consumer Law), filter by language, and book a consultation in minutes.

KEY FEATURES

• Verified Advocates — Every advocate goes through an independent identity and enrolment check before appearing on the platform.

• Book by Chat or Call — Choose your consultation mode and schedule a future slot directly with an active advocate.

• Legal Matter Workspace — Maintain a private, structured record of your legal case including notes, tasks, advice, and court reference numbers (CNR).

• Anonymous Consultation — Start a confidential 24-hour chat without creating an account. Your mobile number is never stored.

• Multi-language Support — Advocates available in English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, Punjabi, Urdu, Bengali and more.

• My Consultations — Track every booking from requested → accepted → completed in one place.

• Grievance Centre — Submit confidential service or safety concerns directly to the platform.

• Privacy by Design — Anonymous chat messages expire after 24 hours. No raw phone numbers stored. Booking data visible only to you and your assigned advocate.

NyayaSetu does not itself provide legal advice. No lawyer-client relationship is created by booking through the platform. Always confirm the scope and terms of any engagement directly with your advocate.
